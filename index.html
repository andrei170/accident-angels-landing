Questions

What accident victims ask us

Are you a law firm or a clinic?+
No. Accident Angels of America is a free 24/7 helpline and referral service. We connect accident victims across Ohio with injury physicians and accident attorneys. We are not a law firm and not a clinic, and Accident Angels of America is not licensed to provide legal advice.
How much does it cost to call?+
Nothing. We provide free 24/7 compassionate support. Call (844) 952-6435 any time, day or night, and we will help you understand your next steps and connect you with the right professionals in your area.
What kinds of accidents do you help with?+
We support people after car, motorcycle, truck, and pedestrian accidents anywhere in Ohio. We help you find an injury physician, connect with an accident attorney, and document your injuries properly.
What areas of Ohio do you serve?+
We serve accident victims across the entire state of Ohio, including Cincinnati, Columbus, Cleveland, Toledo, Akron, and Dayton, through an Ohio-licensed referral network. Call (844) 952-6435 to be connected.
